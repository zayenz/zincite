# Zincite logo concepts

Three exploratory PNG concepts generated with the built-in imagegen tool.
The crystal direction was selected. All concepts use transparent backgrounds.

- `01-element.png`: a teal Zt tile related to Zirium. The fictional number 162 follows the conventional Aufbau extension of group 12; placement beyond known elements is theoretical. A favicon variant should omit the number.
- `02-crystal.png`: an angular orange and charcoal Z inspired by mineral facets.
- `03-code.png`: a blue Z made from broad, aligned strokes.

The briefs ask for bold shapes, few colours and no fine details. The generated
concepts contain some shading; the chosen direction can be redrawn as flat SVG
and optically adjusted for a favicon.

The selected crystal was refined with imagegen in `04-crystal-refined.png`,
then redrawn with five flat polygons in `../zincite-mark.svg` to remove shading
and edge artefacts. PNG exports include the mark at 512px and favicons at 16px
and 32px. The README has not been changed.

## Crystal refinement brief

Refine this selected Zincite logo concept. Preserve its bold angular crystal Z silhouette and the mineral character. Keep it simple: three broad interlocking facets, sharp geometry, generous clear gaps, flat solid colours. Use warm orange and darker rust orange instead of the charcoal diagonal so the entire Z stays visible on both white and dark backgrounds. Remove gradients, shading, tiny facets and stray pixels. The mark must read as a Z at 16px favicon size. Fill most of a square canvas with a small even margin. No text, numbers, border, container, shadows or extra symbols. Transparent background. Return one clean isolated logo, not a mockup or presentation sheet.

## Generation briefs

### 1. element

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one simple logo concept for Zincite, a Rust formatter and linter for MiniZinc. Explore a periodic-table sibling to the existing Zirium logo: Zirium has a dark charcoal rounded square, an orange border, and a very bold white Z with an orange lowercase i. Zincite's symbol is exactly "Zt" (capital Z, lowercase t). Keep that family resemblance but give Zincite its own restrained teal accent. A bold compact Zt fills most of a charcoal rounded square with a thick teal border; small atomic number "162" in the upper left. 162 is a fictional branding choice from the conventional extended group 12 column. Only text: "Zt" and "162". No electron configuration, atomic mass, group label, subtitle, full wordmark, fine details, textures, gradients or shadows. Flat crisp vector-like forms, legible strong strokes, balanced optical spacing. Designed to simplify naturally to a 16px favicon by keeping the Zt as the unmistakable dominant silhouette. One centered isolated mark with modest padding on a transparent background. Send an actual clean logo asset, not a presentation board or mockup.

### 2. crystal

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one simple and distinctive logo concept for Zincite, a Rust formatter and linter for MiniZinc. Direction: a mineral/crystal-inspired angular Z emblem, an alternative to a periodic-table tile. Build a bold Z silhouette out of only two or three broad interlocking geometric crystal facets. A compact clean memorable mark using warm rust orange and deep charcoal. Suggest a mineral through the geometry alone; keep the Z immediately readable. No container, letters beyond the implied Z, words, numbers, microscopic facets, outlines, texture, gradients, shadows or sparkle. Flat crisp vector-like forms. It must remain recognizable as a solid silhouette at 16px favicon size and work in one colour. One centered isolated logo with modest padding on a transparent background. Send an actual clean logo asset, not a presentation board or mockup.

### 3. code

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one simple distinctive logo concept for Zincite, a Rust formatter and linter for MiniZinc. Direction: an abstract typographic Z formed from three broad aligned code-line strokes, conveying orderly source formatting. This is a different idea from a periodic-table tile or mineral crystal. Find a compact clever geometric Z silhouette with a diagonal connector and disciplined spacing, using deep indigo with a restrained bright blue accent. Two colours maximum, very bold broad forms, generous negative space, no thin lines. No square container, literal code characters, words, numbers, checkmarks, extra decorative lines, gradients, shadows or textures. Flat crisp vector-like forms, recognisable at 16px favicon size and workable in one colour. One centered isolated mark with modest padding on a transparent background. Send an actual clean logo asset, not a presentation board or mockup.
